NEON BAY

An open-world crime game. Steal cars, outrun the police, and work off your debt to the docks.
Rio Vale is out of prison and owes Mara Solano of the Neon Bay docks a fortune. Four jobs stand between Rio and freedom: a stolen coupe, a getaway through downtown, five packages across the city, and one last vault. The whole city is open — take a car, any car.
WASD move & drive · Mouse drag camera · Click shoot · E enter / exit car · Shift sprint · Space handbrake · M start next mission · P / Esc pause · progress saves automatically
